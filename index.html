About the 8-Puzzle

A 3×3 board holds tiles 1–8 and one blank. A move slides a neighbouring tile into the blank (up, down, left or right). The task is to reach a goal arrangement from an initial one with the fewest moves. States are board layouts, actions are blank moves, each move costs 1, so it is a classic state-space search problem. Only half of all arrangements are solvable (parity of inversions).
